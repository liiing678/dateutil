Fixed ``relativedelta`` arithmetic for reminders expressed in relative
terms: adding whole months or years to the last day of a month now stays
aligned to the last day of the target month instead of drifting off
month-end or overflowing into the following month; arithmetic on
timezone-aware datetimes now preserves the wall clock time across UTC
offset changes and raises ``ValueError`` when the result would be an
imaginary (nonexistent) local time; invalid absolute arguments such as
``month=0`` or ``day=32`` now raise ``ValueError`` instead of being
silently ignored or clamped to an approximate value.
